The Apartment

Refined alpine chic with a view

Casa Bella sits on the third floor of “Casa Valbella” – a 4.5-room apartment that blends modern design with genuine reclaimed wood, natural stone and a striking antler chandelier. The spacious open living, dining and kitchen area opens directly onto the 73 m² sun terrace.

Three bedrooms and two bathrooms sleep up to eight guests – ideal for families and friends. The master bedroom features its own en-suite bathroom and direct terrace access.

FAQ

Good to know before booking

Is there Wi-Fi in the apartment?
Yes, fast Wi-Fi is available free of charge throughout the apartment – perfect for remote work in the mountains too.
How do I best get to Arosa?
By car via the panoramic road from Chur (about 30 minutes) – the apartment includes 2 garage spaces. Alternatively, the RhB railway brings you comfortably and car-free right into the village. Note Arosa's night-time driving ban.
How many guests can Casa Bella accommodate?
The 142 m² apartment sleeps 1 to 8 guests across 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. Ideal for families or two couples holidaying together in Arosa.
Is there parking at the apartment?
Yes. Casa Bella includes 2 weather-protected garage spaces right by the apartment plus a shared ski room for your gear.
Are pets allowed?
Pets are unfortunately not permitted at Casa Bella.
How central is the apartment in Arosa?
Very central: restaurants, bars and shops are within walking distance and the free local bus stops right outside. The Arosa Lenzerheide ski area with 225 km of slopes is quickly reached.
What are the check-in and check-out times?
Check-in is Saturdays from 4:00 pm, check-out by 10:00 am. Other arrival and departure times are possible on request.
How do I book Casa Bella?
Conveniently online via the Bergluft Arosa website (button “Availability & Booking”). On-site hosting is also provided by Bergluft Arosa.

Arosa Guide

Insider tips for your stay

Skiing & Snowboarding

225 km of slopes in the Arosa Lenzerheide ski area – the lift is just a few minutes' walk away.

Winter hiking & sledding

Around 60 km of prepared winter hiking trails and a fast toboggan run offer variety beyond the slopes.

Hiking & biking

In summer, 300 km of trails, the popular 10-lake hike and bike trails for every level await.

Arosa Bear Sanctuary

The sanctuary for rescued brown bears is a family highlight – just a few minutes away.

Food & dining

From rustic mountain restaurants and fondue parlours to fine dining in the village – there's something for every taste.

Getting here & around

Easily by RhB train from Chur into the low-traffic village – the free local bus takes you everywhere on site.
